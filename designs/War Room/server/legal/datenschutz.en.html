1. Controller

Name, address, e-mail (as in the legal notice)

2. Overview

War Room works without user accounts, cookies, tracking, advertising or third-party services. Fonts and all program code are loaded from this server. Only what is needed to run the planning rooms is processed.

3. Hosting

The application runs on a server at hosting provider name and address, server location, under a data processing agreement (Art. 28 GDPR).

4. Connection data

The server necessarily processes your IP address. It is only used in memory to limit abuse (e.g. rooms created per hour, concurrent connections) and is not stored. No access logs with IP addresses are kept. Legal basis: Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR (secure operation).

5. Planning rooms

The content of a room – title, drawings, planned and conquered provinces, phases, layers, and the nicknames and nations you enter – is stored on the server so everyone with the room link can plan together. An optional room password is only stored as a hash. While you are connected, your nickname, cursor position, map view and pings are sent live to the others in the room but not stored.

Rooms are deleted when their creator deletes them, or automatically after 90 days without changes. Legal basis: Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR (providing the feature you use).

6. Feedback and error reports

When you send feedback we store your text, the optional contact, the page (without room id), language, app version and browser user agent. If the app hits an error, a report with technical details (error text, code location, page without room id, app version, user agent) is sent automatically. This data is only used to improve the test version and is deleted after the test phase. Legal basis: Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR (feedback) and (f) (fixing errors).

7. Storage on your device

The app stores in your browser (local storage, IndexedDB, session storage): your language and view settings, a copy of opened plans (so they work offline), recently opened rooms, which nation you play, a deletion key for rooms you created and – for the current session only – a room password you entered. This data stays on your device and can be cleared in your browser settings. Legal basis: § 25(2) no. 2 TDDDG (strictly necessary for the requested service).

8. Your rights

You have the right of access, rectification, erasure, restriction, data portability and objection (Art. 15–21 GDPR) and may withdraw consent for the future. Contact the address above. You may also lodge a complaint with a data protection supervisory authority.
